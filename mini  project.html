Chocolate Cake Recipe

About Chocolate Cake

Chocolate cake is a sweet and delicious dessert made with cocoa powder.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla essence

Instructions

  1. Take a bowl.
  2. Add flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder.
  3. Add milk, oil, and vanilla essence.
  4. Mix all the ingredients well.
  5. Pour the batter into a prepared cake pan.
  6. Bake in a preheated oven until the cake is cooked.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.

Enjoy your delicious chocolate cake!
